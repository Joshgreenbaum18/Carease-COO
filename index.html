SNF Ancillary Service Context — What This Company Likely Provides

SNF ancillary service providers deliver clinical and support services to skilled nursing facilities — typically on a contract basis. The most common service lines that fast-growing SNF ancillary companies offer:

Therapy Services (PT/OT/SLP)

Physical, occupational, and speech therapy — the largest ancillary cost and revenue driver in most SNFs. Contract therapy companies staff therapists across facilities. Josh's AMDC experience included therapeutic services coordination.

Staffing Services

RN, LPN, CNA, and allied health staffing to SNFs — the fastest-growing ancillary segment post-COVID. Josh managed 250 healthcare employees and understands staffing economics, certification requirements, and labor compliance.

Pharmacy / Infusion Services

Long-term care pharmacy, IV infusion, and specialty medication services to SNF residents. Josh ran Community Care / Orphan Pharmacy — direct pharmacy services to healthcare facility populations.

Diagnostic / Lab Services

Mobile or on-site diagnostic services — X-ray, EKG, lab, ultrasound, wound care assessments. Josh founded Optimum Diagnostics specifically to deliver diagnostic services to SNF and healthcare facility populations.

Behavioral Health / Psychiatric

Geri-psych and psychology services delivered on-site to SNF residents. High-demand, Medicare Part B billing, strong margin. Josh's AMDC background included coordinating behavioral health referrals for a complex medically ill population.

Revenue Cycle / Billing Management

Outsourced billing and revenue cycle management for SNFs. Josh managed Medicaid billing across 7 AMDC locations and ran pharmacy billing at Community Care — understands the full revenue cycle complexity of healthcare facility billing.

What Josh Understands About SNF Administrators That Other COO Candidates Don't
PDPM Revenue Pressure

Post-PDPM, SNFs are focused on acuity mix and clinical quality metrics. An ancillary service COO who understands PDPM knows which services drive facility revenue, which create survey risk, and how to position the company's services as a financial benefit to the facility — not just a cost.

Survey & Compliance Stakes

SNF administrators live in fear of CMS surveys and deficiencies. An ancillary service company that creates any compliance risk will be terminated immediately. Josh operated under DMAHS and CMS regulatory scrutiny for 10 years — he builds service models that pass audits.

Staffing Reality

SNF administrators are desperate for reliable staff and ancillary partners who show up consistently. Josh managed 250 healthcare employees across 7 locations — he understands healthcare workforce dynamics and can build the reliability that makes ancillary companies sticky.

Payer & Billing Complexity

Medicare Part A, Part B, Medicaid, Medicare Advantage — SNF billing is among the most complex in healthcare. Josh managed Medicaid billing across 7 AMDC locations and ran pharmacy billing. He won't be surprised by billing complexity in the COO seat.
